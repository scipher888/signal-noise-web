@natolambert: New slide deck on the biggest open problems in post-training and how the field got to where it is today. Largely, this is a statement of my goals with Trillium Labs (not that we can do every project at once). Stay in touch if you're working on any of these in the open!

Opens with the evolution of post-training and ends with a debate on how RSI is changing this type of research.

The research areas on my mind:

1. Harness specialization and design – How do we design environments for context management that enable new paradigms of using LLMs?
2. Character training – How do we create the personality of models and how does this impact safety, trust, and diffusion of LLMs?
3. Open-source RL environments – How do we create scalable, open data methods?
4. Reasoning efficiency in RL – How do we control and manipulate the reasoning efficiency of LLMs?
5. Scaling laws for RL – How do we design principled experimentation methodology for large-scale post-training?
6. Multi-agent scaling laws – What are the main axes of inquiry into multi-LLM systems and collaborations?
