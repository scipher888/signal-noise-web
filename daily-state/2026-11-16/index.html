Both stories are company reported, and in both cases the primary sources support a narrower claim than the claims circulating around them. On Qwen4-Max, Alibaba's Qwen team presents the model as a more efficient successor to Qwen3.8-Max. The company announcement says Qwen4-Max is built as a mixture of experts model with 2.4 trillion total parameters, activating only 71 billion per token. That is 25 percent fewer activated parameters than Qwen3.8-Max at the same 2.4 trillion scale, according to Alibaba. The company says that design delivers stronger capabilities at a lower inference cost, and that the model is available now on QwenCloud through the Token Plan and the API. Those efficiency and capability claims are company reported. The announcement relies on Alibaba's own evaluation, including a proprietary benchmark called Work360, and those figures have not been independently replicated. That matters for how to read the claims about real world productivity, professional tools, and long horizon execution. They come from controlled internal testing by the developer, not from independent real world use, and they should not be treated as verified outcomes in workplaces or other settings.
